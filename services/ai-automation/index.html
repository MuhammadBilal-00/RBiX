The Problem

Manual follow-up is where revenue quietly leaks out.

A lead comes in from an ad, a form, or a DM. If nobody replies within a few minutes, the odds of ever converting that lead start falling by the hour — and most small teams simply can't staff round-the-clock response. An appointment gets booked and nobody calls to confirm it, so a chair, a table, or a technician's morning sits empty. An invoice goes out and sits unchased for weeks because nobody owns the follow-up. A vendor invoice arrives as a PDF and someone re-types it into the accounting system by hand, one line at a time, every single day.

None of this is a strategy problem. It's a systems problem — the business is running on people remembering to do things, at the exact moments people are busiest doing something else. That's the gap RBiX closes first, before touching anything else.

Our Approach

We build against real leads, real appointments, real invoices — from week one.

No mockups, no six-month roadmap before anything ships. Every engagement follows the same three-step path.

1

Map the workflow

We trace exactly where a lead, appointment, or invoice currently gets stuck, delayed, or dropped — and agree what "automated" needs to mean for your business specifically.

2

Build against the real thing

The automation goes live against your actual inbound leads, real appointments, or real invoices — not a staging environment or a demo dataset.

3

Monitor, tag, refine

Every conversation and outcome is logged and tagged, so the flow gets sharper each week and the reporting layer has something real to measure.

What's Included

Seven automations, deployed individually or as a stack.

Every engagement starts with whichever of these is costing you the most today.

Lead Response Engine

Every inbound lead — from an ad, web form, or social/search message — receives a qualified WhatsApp or SMS reply within 60 seconds, 24/7. An AI-driven flow asks 2–3 qualifying questions, tags the lead hot/warm/cold in the CRM, and routes it to the right person, with automatic escalation if no one responds in 10 minutes.

  • Best for: any business relying on inbound leads from ads, forms, or social/search

No-Show Reduction Flow

A scheduled reminder sequence (48h, 24h, and 2h before an appointment) with one-tap confirm/reschedule, so missed appointments drop without staff having to manually call or text.

  • Best for: clinics, salons, and any appointment-based business

E-commerce Recovery Suite

Automatically recovers abandoned carts with a follow-up message and product images, and confirms cash-on-delivery orders before dispatch to cut failed deliveries.

  • Best for: e-commerce brands and online stores taking COD or facing cart abandonment

Invoice & Receipt OCR

A photo or PDF of an invoice/receipt — uploaded or forwarded by WhatsApp/email — is read by OCR and AI extraction, validated against basic rules, and pushed straight into accounting or compliance systems, with anything uncertain flagged for a human.

  • Best for: SMEs and bookkeepers automating manual data entry and e-invoicing/tax compliance

Payment Chaser

Automatically chases overdue invoices with escalating reminders and payment links at day 1, 7, 14, and 30 overdue, following a pre-approved script, then flags anything still unpaid to the owner.

  • Best for: B2B businesses and practices managing overdue receivables

Document Expiry & Compliance Tracker

Tracks licences, insurance, certifications, and filing deadlines against today's date, with escalating alerts at 60/30/14/3 days before anything lapses.

  • Best for: businesses tracking regulatory deadlines, renewals, or compliance filings across many clients or assets

Quote Follow-Up Sequence

Automatically works cold quotes for 30–90 days with a scheduled, varied follow-up cadence, pulling the lead out of sequence and alerting the assigned person the moment they reply.

  • Best for: contractors and B2B businesses with quotes that go cold before closing
