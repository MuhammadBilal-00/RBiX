The Problem

The website does nothing, and the back office runs on guesswork.

Most small and growing businesses are running on a website that hasn't changed in years — static pages, no clear path to actually talk to the business, and zero leads to show for it. It sits there as a digital brochure while the real work of winning customers happens somewhere else, or doesn't happen at all.

Behind the scenes it's worse. Staff re-enter the same customer details into a spreadsheet, then a booking tool, then an invoicing app, because none of them talk to each other. The owner has five different logins and zero single report that tells them what's actually happening in the business today. Every new SaaS subscription papers over one gap and opens two more. None of this needs more tools bolted on — it needs one system built around how the business actually runs.

Our Approach

We build against your real records and real traffic, from week one.

No throwaway prototypes and no guessing at what the business actually needs. Every build follows the same three-step path.

1

Audit what exists

We map every spreadsheet, tool, and page that's actually load-bearing in the business today, before designing anything that replaces it.

2

Build around real data

The system goes live against your real customer records, real traffic, and real transactions — migrated in, not recreated from a demo.

3

Ship, then extend

The core system launches live and in use, then gets extended module by module as the business's actual needs prove themselves out.

What's Included

Six builds, deployed individually or as a stack.

Every engagement starts with whichever gap is costing you the most today.

Conversion-Focused Business Website

A fast, mobile-first site built to turn visitors into direct conversations rather than page views, with every key page carrying a clear contact/enquiry path wired to the Lead Response Engine and automatic campaign/source attribution.

  • Best for: any business whose current site is outdated, static, or generating no leads

E-commerce Storefront

A branded storefront (Salla, Zid, Shopify, or headless) fully integrated with local payment and shipping options, syncing every order automatically into the E-commerce Recovery Suite and the reporting layer.

  • Best for: retail and product brands moving into or scaling online sales

Practice / Clinic Management System

Manages patients or customers, appointments, staff, and billing in one place — records follow the customer across every visit, appointments sync to a shared calendar, and billing generates directly from the appointment record.

  • Best for: multi-branch clinics, salons, and appointment-based service groups replacing spreadsheets

Broker / Lead Desk (CRM)

Pulls leads automatically from external portals or channels, qualifies and routes them by rule (area, budget, listing), schedules viewings/meetings directly into calendars, and rolls performance up into a weekly dashboard.

  • Best for: real estate brokerages and sales-driven businesses managing high lead volume

Internal Operations Dashboard (ERP-Lite)

Connects or migrates existing spreadsheets, tools, and SaaS subscriptions into a single structured layer, so staff enter data once and the owner gets one login and one report instead of five disconnected ones.

  • Best for: SMEs outgrowing spreadsheets or running several disconnected SaaS tools

Client Self-Service Portal

A secure web portal where clients log in with a phone number or email to view history, upcoming appointments/deadlines, invoices, and documents, upload files, and pay outstanding balances — all updating the same central system staff use.

  • Best for: practices and firms wanting clients to self-serve instead of calling in

Why It Matters

Custom software earns its place — it isn't the starting point.

  • It replaces a workflow that's already proven itself.We build custom software once a process has been running under automation and has clearly outgrown it — not as a guess at what the business might need.
  • One system instead of another subscription.Rather than adding a sixth tool to the pile, the build consolidates the spreadsheets and SaaS logins that already exist into a single structured layer.
  • Built around your process, not a template.The system is shaped by how your team actually books, bills, and serves customers today — not bent to fit a generic off-the-shelf workflow.
  • Scales cleanly as the business grows.The same structure holds as you add locations, staff, or transaction volume, instead of needing to be re-built every time the business takes a step up.
