The Problem

Paper and phone calls don't survive contact with the field.

Inspectors, technicians, contractors, and distributed retail or hospitality staff spend their day where signal is unreliable or simply absent — basements, rural sites, warehouses, remote job locations. Paper checklists get left in a van, filled in from memory hours later, or lost entirely. Photos taken on a personal phone carry no verifiable timestamp or location, so there's no real proof of when a job was actually done or where.

Meanwhile managers have no real-time view of who's where and what's actually finished — status updates happen by phone call, if they happen at all. A generic consumer app doesn't match a specific inspection checklist or roster structure, and most "no-code app builders" simply stop working the moment the connection drops mid-task, which is exactly when field staff need them most.

Our Approach

Built around the field workflow you already run — proven on a pilot team first.

No generic app shell retrofitted to your checklist. Every mobile engagement follows the same three-step path.

1

Map the exact field workflow

We sit with the checklist, roster, or job structure your team already uses on paper or in their heads, and translate it exactly — no generic template standing in for how your inspections or shifts actually run.

2

Build offline-first from day one

Offline support isn't bolted on afterward — the app is architected from the start to capture checklist entries, photos, and status with zero connection, then sync automatically once signal returns.

3

Pilot, then roll out wider

The app goes live with one field team or site first, so real jobs surface any gaps before it's rolled out across the rest of your staff or locations.

What's Included

Two apps, built around field work and distributed staff.

A focused pair rather than a long feature list — each one built specifically for how your team actually works.

Field Service & Inspection App

An offline-first app for site visits, inspections, and maintenance jobs. Staff work through a structured checklist with no internet connection required — nothing waits on a signal bar. Photos are automatically GPS- and time-stamped as they're taken, and everything syncs to the central system the moment the device is back in range.

  • Best for: property management, construction, and field-service businesses needing documented inspections

Staff Operations App

Gives shift or field staff their roster, tasks, and communications in one place. Staff clock in and out, mark tasks complete, and flag issues with photos directly from the app, while managers see real-time completion status without having to call around to find out what's actually been done.

  • Best for: multi-branch or multi-location businesses coordinating distributed staff

How Offline-First Actually Works

A job done with zero bars still shows up correctly timestamped.

No special network, no manual "upload" step — just a straightforward sequence built to assume the connection isn't there.

1

Loaded before the visit

The checklist and any reference data the job needs are stored on the device before staff ever leave for the site — nothing has to be fetched once they're out in the field.

2

Captured locally, no connection needed

Every action — checklist entries, photos with GPS and timestamp attached — is recorded straight to the device. None of it depends on a live connection to go through.

3

Queued, then synced automatically

The app queues everything that was captured offline and syncs it automatically the moment signal returns — there's no manual "upload now" button for staff to remember.

4

Dashboards update on sync

Office and management dashboards update the instant sync completes, so a job finished in a basement with zero bars still lands correctly timestamped and in the right place.
